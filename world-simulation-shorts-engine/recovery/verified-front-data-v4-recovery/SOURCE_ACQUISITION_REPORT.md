The CMH21–2 volume contains seven official Army maps with dated observed UN front legends. Original projection/datum and a numeric representative-fraction scale are not printed. Graphic scale bars and one latitude parallel do not supply a known original CRS. Map6 is PDF index124, printed114; map7 is index286, printed276. Their native, unresampled rasters, original document hash, extraction recipes, separate raster hashes and source-review evidence are permanent repository files. Map7 was independently re-extracted byte-exactly.

Only Army-authored map rasters are redistributed here under the documented U.S. Government work basis. The whole book is not asserted public domain; its photographs have mixed sources. Full original PDFs remain at their official archival URLs, with exact original hashes recorded. Runtime and CPU reproduction require the committed native map rasters and reference data, not a `/tmp` PDF or a live download.

Early invasion arrows and ambiguous phase lines in CMH19–6 are also excluded from actual-front import.
