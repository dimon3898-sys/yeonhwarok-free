Actual software WebGL2 `draw` and `readPixels` are required. A must match the old F raw RGBA hashes at frames 0, 75 and 149. B/C/D use identical event, scene, timeline and camera conditions except their declared opt-in feature. E/F camera conditions are recorded separately. Synthetic GPU fixtures isolate actual ownership classification, effective source material, coast/domain union, NoData and composition effects where moving-camera screenshots cannot measure an individual cause.

Every result labels CPU assertions, actual software GPU pixels, mock tests, and unexecuted NVIDIA checks separately. A brighter screenshot alone does not certify geography, alignment or final visual quality. White moving control front and gold outer domain boundary are separate masks and are checked separately.

## Parameter obligations

The production contract must describe all ten requested controls: Territory Color Strength, Territory Transition Timing, Frontier Glow Intensity, Frontier Glow Width, Terrain Contrast, ASTRA Edge Blending, Unit Marker Scale, Camera Focus Priority, Camera Motion Smoothing, and Event Hold Duration. Defaults must preserve old F when the new profile is absent. Bounded supported controls are validated. Unsupported event retiming, per-pixel acquisition age, or story hold must be explicitly rejected or documented as unavailable; a camera reading pause must not be described as a paused event.

## Persistence and execution boundary

Implement stages in A–J order and save verified stages before longer validation. Only additive files in the new namespace and a separate workflow may change. Parent files, original source samples, geometry, event sequence, artwork, approved video and image remain byte-identical. A new job ID, source signature and claim namespace must preserve old job history and the common GPU exclusion lock.

Use standard free GitHub-hosted Ubuntu runners only, gated on the public repository and sufficient disk. Build from the immutable parent, publish a unique tag, independently pull its digest, and verify the exact source inventory. No local low-space image build, existing-image overwrite, GPU deployment, paid run, or FINAL promotion is authorized. Actual NVIDIA and mobile MP4 review remain subsequent user-approved checks.
